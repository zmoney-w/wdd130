About Us

At Wild Current Rafting, our mission is simple: get you on the water and keep you smiling. Whether you're a first-timer looking for a taste of adventure or a seasoned paddler chasing Class V thrills, we craft experiences that push your limits and leave you wanting more. Safety is our foundation. Fun is our promise. The river is our home — and we'd love to share it with you.

History

Wild Current Rafting was founded in 2003 by two lifelong river enthusiasts who wanted to share their passion for white water with their community. What started as a single raft and a borrowed truck has grown into a full-service outfitter with a fleet of modern equipment, a team of certified guides, and thousands of happy adventurers each season. Over two decades on the water, we've learned that the river teaches something different every single day — and we never stop learning alongside our guests.
