Our Trips

Whether you are looking for a relaxed scenic float with the family or a full-throttle multi-day plunge through canyon rapids, we have a trip that was made for you. All trips include certified guide service, safety gear, and a story worth telling.

Half Day Scenic Float

4 hrs Class I–II Ages 5+ $65/person

Our most popular family trip. Drift through breathtaking canyon scenery, splash through splashy Class II riffles, and take in the wildlife along the riverbank. No experience needed — just show up ready to have fun. Snacks and a guide provided throughout.

Full Day Whitewater Run

8 hrs Class III–IV Ages 12+ $130/person

This is the real thing. You will tackle some of Idaho's most exciting named rapids — Big Mallard, Tumble Creek, and more — with a certified guide who knows every line on the river. A riverside lunch is included. Prior paddling experience is helpful but not required.

3-Day Salmon River Expedition

3 days Class IV–V Ages 16+ $495/person

For the serious adventurer. Over three days you will run the legendary River of No Return through remote canyon wilderness, camp on sandy beaches under the stars, and tackle Class V drops including the infamous Los Dientes del Diablo. All camping gear, meals, and experienced guide service included. Participants must be comfortable in Class III water at minimum.
