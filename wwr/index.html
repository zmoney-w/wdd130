Why Paddle With Us?

Expert-Led Adventures

Every trip is led by a certified Swift Water Rescue guide with years of river experience. We brief you fully before launch, watch over you every rapid of the way, and make sure you come home with a great story.

Trips for Every Level

Whether this is your first time in a raft or you have been chasing Class V drops for years, we have a run that fits. Our trip menu spans calm scenic floats all the way to multi-day wilderness expeditions deep in the canyon.

Top-of-the-Line Gear

We invest in the best self-bailing rafts, dry bags, wetsuits, and helmets on the market so you can focus entirely on the river. Our equipment is inspected and refreshed every season, no questions asked.

Built Around Safety

Safety is not an afterthought for us — it is the foundation everything else is built on. From pre-trip safety briefings to in-water rescue drills, our team trains year-round so you never have to worry about anything but having fun.
